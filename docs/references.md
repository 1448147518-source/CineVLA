# 参考文献

- **π³**: *Scalable Permutation-Equivariant Visual Geometry Learning*, ICLR 2026. [arXiv:2507.13347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13347) — 纯相对位姿监督 + 测地线旋转 + Huber 平移
- **LingBot-Map**: *Geometric Context Transformer for Streaming 3D Reconstruction*, 2026. [arXiv:2604.14141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14141) — c2w 绝对位姿 + 因果相对位姿 + 渐进训练
- **VGGT**: *Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer*, CVPR 2025 (Best Paper). [arXiv:2503.11651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11651) — 首个显式相机参数监督的 feed-forward 重建模型
- **Kendall & Cipolla**: *Geometric Loss Functions for Camera Pose Regression with Deep Learning*, CVPR 2017. — 可学习同方差不确定性加权
- **Hempel et al.**: *Toward Robust and Unconstrained Full Range of Rotation Head Pose Estimation*, IEEE TIP 2024. — 6D 连续旋转表示 + 测地线 loss
- **Lee & Civera**: *Robust Single Rotation Averaging Revisited*, 2024. [arXiv:2309.05388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05388) — Chordal vs Geodesic 距离关系分析
- **DUSt3R**: Wang et al., *DUSt3R: Geometric 3D Vision Made Easy*, CVPR 2024. [arXiv:2312.14132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14132) — 点图回归范式，隐式相机恢复
- **PoseDiffusion**: Wang et al., *Solving Pose Estimation via Diffusion-aided Bundle Adjustment*, ICCV 2023. [arXiv:2306.15667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15667) — 扩散模型做 SfM
